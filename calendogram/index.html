Voice to-do list for building sites

Calendogram

Calendogram keeps tasks by person, place, project and date, with the colour legend of the Excel sheet it replaces.

On site you just talk: the note is written down and split into dated tasks with the right person, ready to review.

Open the app →rokogrga.com ↗

What it shows

  • Excel-like grid on PC, a list on the phone. Click a cell to edit, Tab to move; group by person, place, project, date or colour.
  • Projects with offers. Ask several firms for an offer, pick one, and the others are marked not chosen (see the example house "Ivica").
  • Voice notes. In the real app OpenAI writes the note down and splits it into tasks you review before saving, in Croatian, English or German.
  • Offline first. Everything works without signal and syncs through a small server when it comes back.

About this demo

The demo is the real app with the server switched off: sample tasks and an example project, saved only in this browser. Settings → Log out resets it.

Voice here uses your browser's own dictation and makes one task per note. The AI sorting needs the server, so it is shown in the video instead.
