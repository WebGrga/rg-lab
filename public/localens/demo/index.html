  • CriticalCloudflare blocks ChatGPT search crawlersImpact 5/5Effort 1/5

    Why it matters

    OAI-SearchBot and GPTBot receive a Cloudflare challenge page instead of your site. If ChatGPT search cannot read your pages, it cannot quote or cite them.

    What to do

    In Cloudflare, open Security > Bots and allow OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User and GPTBot, or switch AI crawler blocking to 'allow search crawlers'.

    Evidence
    • Page
      www.poliklinika-lucic.example/
      Observed
      OAI-SearchBot: 403 with Cloudflare challenge. GPTBot: 403 with Cloudflare challenge.
      Expected
      HTTP 200 with the normal page for search and user-request crawlers.
      Excerpt
      HTTP 403 - Just a moment... (Cloudflare challenge)
  • MediumThree pages share the same titleImpact 3/5Effort 1/5
