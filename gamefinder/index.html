Trend radar for YouTube creators

Gamefinder

Gamefinder ranks indie games by how good an opportunity they are for a YouTube creator, not by raw popularity.

Every score explains itself: rising attention and build potential add points, a crowded topic takes them away.

Try the radar ↓Code on GitHub ↗

The radar

Fictional sample games. Pick one, then change its signals.

    What it shows

    • Explainable scoring. Each point in the score comes from a named rule you can read, not a black box.
    • Saturation awareness. Push a game past 35 recent uploads and it loses points: demand without competition is the target.
    • Creator output. The scores turn into video angles a creator could record today.
    • Offline first. The real tool collects from Steam, Reddit and optionally YouTube into SQLite, and also runs fully offline.

    About this demo

    The radar on this page runs the same scoring rules as the Python tool, ported to the browser, on fictional sample games. Nothing is fetched from Steam, YouTube or Reddit.

    Drag the sliders or toggle tags and watch the ranking and the reasons change.
