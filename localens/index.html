AI and local visibility audits

Localens

Localens checks how a local business shows up when customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google, and in Maps and search.

It ends with a report an agency can hand to its client: who AI names instead of you, and the fixes ordered by payoff.

Open the sample report →Code on GitHub ↗

What it shows

  • Honest numbers. Results are ranges across repeated runs, and a part that was not measured says so instead of showing zero.
  • Share of voice. Mention, recommendation and citation are counted separately, against the competitors AI names.
  • A fix plan, not a score dump. Fixes are ranked by expected effect divided by effort, starting with things like AI crawlers blocked at Cloudflare.
  • Spend control. Every paid call is priced before it runs, capped by a budget and recorded.

About this demo

The sample report is the app's own report view, rendered once from the repo's fictional fixture clinic. The AI numbers are illustrative and nothing runs live here, so no keys and no costs.

The full app is Next.js with a Postgres database, a background audit worker and an MCP server; see the repo to run it.
